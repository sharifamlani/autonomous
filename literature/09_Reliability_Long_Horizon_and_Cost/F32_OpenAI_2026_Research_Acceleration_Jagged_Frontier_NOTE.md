## What it says (quoted from the page)

- Estimated autonomous success for two-to-four-hour tasks rose "from 23% to 46%" between March and October; for four-to-eight-hour tasks from 13% to 35%.
- By task: autonomous success 79% for monitoring training runs, 35% for launching training runs, 25% for writing inference and serving code.
- Agent workdays per researcher rose "from 0.5 agent workdays per researcher to 5.7 agent workdays per researcher"; median researcher daily usage "reached $2,400 in October, up 62% from September".
- Experiments logged per active experimenter "averaged 1.68 times the 2025 baseline". The report itself notes that activity measures such as lines of code and experiment counts "do not necessarily imply faster research progress".
- Companion post, https://openai.com/index/research-acceleration-view-inside-openai/ (2026-09-06, also fetched): an agentic classifier applied only to tasks with a ground-truth outcome; difficulty proxied by estimated human time; OpenAI calls its measurement "preliminary"; and "In the last 6 months, over half of successful 4-8 hour tasks involved 1 or more interventions".
