## What it says (quoted from the page)

- Six models were run on an updated Epoch Automation Reports benchmark: tasks from Epoch's own work (AI data-center analysis, a "chess variants" research report, a "hidden messages" SVG encoding study, an open-ended Data Insight), graded manually against internal standards where 100% is the standard expected of an Epoch employee (fetch summary wording).
- Overall: GPT-6.2 Astra averaged 65%, "17 points below Fable 5.2" despite a roughly tied ECI score; no model met the employee standard on any task (fetch summary wording). On the data-center task Fable 5.2 scored 97.5% and Opus 5.5 87.5% against 65% for GPT-6.2 Astra; a contractor typically spends about 16 hours per data center, Opus 5.5 took just under 3 hours and Fable 5.2 about 6.5.
- Self-preference: all models preferred their own output over the human gold standard except one (Gemini 4 Argon, on the hidden-messages task).
- Limitations stated: each model run once per task on a limited suite so scores are noisy; many criteria require judgment; setup changed from the previous report so scores are only roughly comparable; tasks may be retired if models have likely seen them in training.
