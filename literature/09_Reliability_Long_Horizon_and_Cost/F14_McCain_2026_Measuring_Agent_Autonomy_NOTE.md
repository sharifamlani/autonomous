## What it says (quoted from the page)

- Longest turns: "the 99.9th percentile turn duration nearly doubled, from under 25 minutes to over 45
  minutes" (October 2025 to January 2026). The median turn is "around 45 seconds". This is turn
  duration inside interactive Claude Code sessions, not a whole unattended run.
- Success and intervention: "Claude Code's success rate on internal users' most challenging tasks
  doubled" and "the average number of human interventions per session decreased from 5.4 to 3.3"
  (internal Anthropic usage, August to December).
- Oversight by experience: "Newer users (<50 sessions) employ full auto-approve roughly 20% of the
  time"; "by 750 sessions, this increases to over 40% of sessions". New users interrupt "in 5% of
  turns", experienced users "in around 9% of turns".
- Agent-initiated stops: "On the most complex tasks, Claude Code asks for clarification more than
  twice as often as on minimal-complexity tasks".

## Limits (the authors')

- "We can only analyze traffic from a single model provider: Anthropic."
- "Many of our strongest findings are grounded in data from Claude Code, and may not generalize to
  other domains or products." "Our classifications are generated by Claude." "we cannot manually
  inspect the underlying data due to privacy constraints." The window is "late 2025 through early
  2026".
