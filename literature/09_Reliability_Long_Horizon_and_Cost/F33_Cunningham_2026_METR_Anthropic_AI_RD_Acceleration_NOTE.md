## What it says (quoted from the page)

- Anthropic is likely improving its models on well-specified tasks at "around twice the rate they did between March 2024 and October 2025", uncertainty range about 1.3x to 3x; benchmark scores grew at 1.9x the earlier rate (95% CI 1.5-2.8x).
- METR guesses "perhaps ≈80%" of the acceleration is due to AI; informal Slack polls reached "≈12x" self-reported uplift, which METR calls "only very loose evidence" (fetch summary wording).
- AI is not yet making "highly significant and fully autonomous contributions" to the most important parts of model training; acceleration comes mainly from AI assistance; gains may be limited to well-specified, automatically graded tasks.
- Method (fetch summary wording): high-access evaluation, 12 August to 29 October 2026, about 2.5 FTE; piecewise-linear fits with a breakpoint at 7 October 2025, interviews with about 15 staff, internal surveys and data. Anthropic redacted most of the evidence METR found most informative, so readers cannot fully check the conclusions, and METR relied heavily on Claude for data analysis.
